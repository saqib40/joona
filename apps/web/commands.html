Commands

Every keyboard shortcut and menu command, for Windows and macOS. The keys for your platform are highlighted.

Dictation

Works in any app while Joona is running.

Action Windows Your platform macOS Your platform
Dictate Hold while you speak, then let go to type where your cursor is. CtrlWin CtrlCmd
Hands-free Double-tap the dictation shortcut to keep talking without holding it. Tap once more to finish. CtrlWin CtrlCmd
Hands-free with Space Start hands-free without double-tapping. You can turn this off in Settings. CtrlWinSpace CtrlCmdSpace
Cancel Stop without typing anything. Esc Esc
Alternate language Dictate in your second output language, if you've set one in Languages. CtrlWinShift CtrlCmdShift
Command Mode Experimental Select text, then hold the shortcut and say how to change it. With nothing selected, Joona writes what you ask for. Turn it on in Settings › Experimental. CtrlWinAlt CtrlCmdOption
Paste last dictation Type your last dictation again, for when it landed in the wrong place. AltShiftZ OptionShiftZ

Joona Bar

The small bar at the bottom of the screen.

  • Click the bar

    Start hands-free. If Joona is paused, this resumes it instead.

  • Right-click the bar

    Open the bar menu to hide it for an hour, pause or resume Joona, open Joona, or change the language.

  • Language chip

    Pick the language you speak, or open Language settings. It appears when you have more than one language or a translation.

Menu bar and tray

The menu bar on macOS, the system tray on Windows.

  • Open Joona

    Open the Hub to see your history and settings.

  • Start hands-free

    Begin dictating without holding the shortcut.

  • Paste last transcript

    Type your last dictation again.

  • Language

    Choose the language you speak, or open Language settings.

  • Pause Joona

    Stop the shortcut from doing anything until you resume.

  • Check for updates

    Check GitHub now. The label changes when an update is ready.

  • Quit Joona

    Close Joona and its speech engine.

Spoken commands

Say these while you dictate. They work on both platforms.

  • “New line” or “Next line”

    Start a new line.

  • “New paragraph”

    Start a new paragraph.

  • “Press enter” Experimental

    Say it at the end of a dictation to press Enter and send the message. Turn it on in Settings › Experimental.

  • Your snippets

    Say a phrase you saved in Settings › Snippets to type its text.

Good to know

Change any shortcut

Open Settings › General to change the dictation and paste-last shortcuts, or turn double-tap and Space for hands-free on or off.

Other keys cancel

Press another key within a second of starting and Joona cancels, passing the key to the app. Your usual shortcuts keep working.

macOS permission

Allow Joona in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility so it can see the shortcut in other apps.
