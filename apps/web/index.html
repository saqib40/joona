What you can do with Joona

Voice typing, local by default

Transcribe on your PC with Whisper, Parakeet or Moonshine. Your words are typed where your cursor is.

Speak one language, type another

Say it in Hindi and get English, or Hinglish in Roman script. Joona transcribes it for you.

Command Mode

Hold Ctrl+Win+Alt and say how to change the selected text, or ask for something new to be written.

Free, open, and yours

No account, no word limits, no telemetry. MIT licensed — see the source on GitHub.
